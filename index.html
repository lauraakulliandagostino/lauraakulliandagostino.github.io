Laura Akullian D’Agostino, Ph.D.Vice President, Chemistry at Monimoi Therapeutics

Laura Akullian D’Agostino, Ph.D., is Vice President of Chemistry at Monimoi Therapeutics. Over two decades at Mersana, Avila, Celgene, Bristol Myers Squibb, and Dunad, she has led chemistry for programs in oncology, immunology, neurodegeneration, and cell therapy, working across small molecules, covalent modulators, protein degraders, and antibody-drug conjugates.

Laura trained as a synthetic organic chemist, working on enantioselective catalysis with Amir Hoveyda for her Ph.D. at Boston College, after a B.S. in chemistry at SUNY Oswego with research in natural product isolation.
